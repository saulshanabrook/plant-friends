Reference photos

Photos come from the verified PFAF photo archive. They retain individual ownership and per-plant credits; missing credits do not imply unrestricted use. Images were resized to at most 640 pixels and converted to WebP. Original URLs, hashes and freshness are preserved in the photo manifest. Photo terms are separate from the PFAF text and software licenses.

BioCLIP 2

The pinned BioCLIP 2 model is MIT licensed. Its complete image encoder was converted to ONNX with FP16 matrix storage and FP32 arithmetic. Scientific-name vectors use the same checkpoint and official prompt ensemble. Model output is a possible catalog match, not proof of identity or safety.
